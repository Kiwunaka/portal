## Devices and Protocols

- Device creation asks for a name and OS: Android, iPhone, Windows, macOS or Other.
- Device card exposes one subscription link covering multiple countries and a settings branch.
- Settings: rename, change OS, subscription/per-server mode, delete and close.
- Delete confirmation copy is “Взрывай дядя” / “Я передумал”.
- A temporary Android device was created, renamed in place, switched to per-server mode and deleted through confirmation. A fresh device list showed only the original device and one free slot.
- Protocols: VlessGrpc, Trojan, xVless, Vless, Outline and WireGuard.
- All protocol selectors were exercised; VlessGrpc was restored.
- Private proxies are gated to subscribed returning customers.

## Payment, Gifts and Referral

## Strengths

- Memorable three-step pricing.
- Broad protocol recovery in the bot.
- Full device lifecycle and private-proxy upsell.
- Russian, English and Chinese localization.

## Weaknesses

- Plan switches without confirmation and without clarifying whether money will be charged later.
- Telegram channel is not indexed by TGStat, so no verified ERR/growth analytics are available.
- Heavy ideological and meme copy can weaken trust.
- Device management is capable but visually noisy and conceptually technical.

## POKROV Implication

Expose protocol recovery and device lifecycle, but require confirmation for plan changes and translate protocol choices into symptom-based guidance.
