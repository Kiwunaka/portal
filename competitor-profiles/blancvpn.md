Seven posts in the two-month window covered product differentiation, a major block, public status, seven-day compensation, own iOS/macOS/Android apps, Happ/Karing/Incy fallbacks and a roadmap for split tunneling, Xray, family access and Windows. A 66% promotion and referral were attached to product material rather than replacing it.

The app and official site are tied to Yadda OÜ. Android was updated 2026-06-16.

## Assessment

BlancVPN is not the fastest grower, but it has the cleanest mature product communication in this wave. Copy the incident/status/compensation/roadmap pattern; do not confuse high reach from rare posts with rapid customer growth.
