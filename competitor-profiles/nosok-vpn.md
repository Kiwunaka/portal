## Product

- Mandatory @NosokVPN channel gate.
- Trial, profile, device connection, QR, gifts, partner program and service information.
- Paid constructor supports 1–10 devices.

- Six-day Telegram-only fallback after subscription expiry.

## Strengths

- Large distribution and measurable cross-promo.
- Strong recovery fallback.
- Full web account plus direct multi-platform installation.
- Flexible device pricing, gifts and a commercially aggressive partner engine.

## Weaknesses

- Telegram Web iframe compatibility is broken even though the direct page works.
- Price, location and device claims conflict across surfaces.
- Partner leaderboard counts and broad scale claims are not independently validated.
- Copy QA is visibly weak.

## Implications for POKROV

Take the recovery fallback, flexible cabinet and direct install matrix. Avoid mandatory gates, contradictory claims and unbounded “passive income” framing.
