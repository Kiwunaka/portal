## Growth And Retention Mechanics

- Five-day trial in current bot/store/app, with historical three-day TV guest flow and other trial lengths visible in reviews.
- Referral days and shareable subscription framing.
- Short-lived promo codes tied to news/blocking moments, including apology/compensation codes.
- Recurring prize draws using the channel subscribe + **Участвую** interaction; prizes range from subscription days to phones and cash.
- Public winner lists and anti-scam warnings around the only account allowed to contact winners.
- Compensation days after outages and occasional discounts/free extensions praised in reviews.
- A separate Telegram proxy microsite used as a free acquisition/utility wedge.
- A review-reward bot accepts Telegram ID or email so bonus days can be credited.

One current Play review alleged non-delivery of a prize. The channel's public winner/result posts show that result publication exists, but they do not independently prove delivery to each winner; neither claim should be converted into a confirmed fraud/fulfillment finding.

## Store-Policy Risk

The channel repeatedly offers subscription days in exchange for App Store/Google Play ratings or reviews. Google Play's current Ratings, Reviews, and Installs policy says developers must not offer incentives of any kind in exchange for ratings or reviews: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898684.

This is direct first-party evidence of a policy-risk acquisition tactic, not an inference. The same pattern appears in sibling publisher product Огонь, suggesting a portfolio-level growth playbook.

## Strategic Read

Батя does not behave like a single app. It uses a resilient distribution graph: native Android/TV and iOS apps, generic key-import clients on desktop, Telegram bot/channel, standalone cabinet, support widget, knowledge base, direct APK sideloading and a free Telegram proxy. When one node is threatened, the channel moves users to another.

The strongest reusable ideas are the channel-as-status-page, compensation automation, purpose-labelled routes, cabinet outside Telegram, TV QR pairing and visible change communication. The practices not to copy are incentivized store reviews, threat/FOMO overuse, contradictory legal/support claims, branded flows on unrelated domains and public endpoint exposure in store creatives.
