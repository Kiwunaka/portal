- Plans: one month, three months, twelve months and Lifetime.
- Lifetime means only while the service remains operational and technically/legally available.
- iOS, Android, Windows, macOS and Android TV are supported; routers are not.
- Access is delivered as VPN keys/configurations through Telegram, direct import or manual import.
- Payments are in RUB through Telegram/payment infrastructure including YooKassa.
- Timed plans may auto-renew; cancellation is via Telegram before the next charge.
- Recommended maximum is five devices / five simultaneous connections.
- 20 Mbps is a non-binding normal-conditions reference.
- China, Iran, Turkmenistan, Mexico and Bangladesh are named as potentially unstable/unavailable.
- Refunds may be considered for technical non-delivery; diagnostics/payment evidence can be requested.
- Support is described as 24/7 in Russian/English, but the written response limit is up to two working days.
- Russian law governs; the offer is effective 2026-03-01.
