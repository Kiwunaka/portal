### Creative-system observations

- A strict navy/yellow system, large white headline and a yellow highlighted phrase make every frame recognizable before the UI is read.
- The sequence moves from promise to product proof to third-party trust, then repeats for phone/tablet/TV rather than treating alternate devices as a footer claim.
- Real UI states do most of the work: connected metrics, country load, favorite stars, Wi-Fi automation and service-specific streaming entries.
- `Private Browser` is cross-sold inside the connection flow, not only on the publisher page.
- Several screens appear older than the installed 8.40 UI and contain stale/contradictory entitlement details. The design system is consistent, but the evidence layer is not fully synchronized with current pricing/device claims.

The iPhone creative story is almost identical to the Android phone sequence. CyberGhost is operating one reusable campaign system across stores, while adapting the device frame and platform UI. This is efficient and recognizable, but the stale account/date/device data should be cleaned before copying the tactic.

## What is worth borrowing

- One visual grammar across app, legal pages and stores: navy foundation, yellow action/highlight, mascot/security metaphor.
- A six-frame acquisition story: privacy outcome, technical proof, no-logs/Wi-Fi proof, network breadth, one-tap ease, external trust.
- Show concrete product affordances—favorites, load, streaming purpose, protocol, automation—rather than only generic shield art.
- Produce separate phone/tablet/TV evidence sets from one campaign template.
- Cross-sell an adjacent utility only where it naturally follows a successful connection.

## What not to copy

- Do not publish stale usernames, renewal dates, internal plan labels, public IPs or contradictory device limits in store creatives.
- Do not use generic release notes for every version if a meaningful user-facing change exists.
- Do not claim accessibility maturity without declaring and testing actual supported features.
