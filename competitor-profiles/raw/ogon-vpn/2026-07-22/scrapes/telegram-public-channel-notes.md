## Readout

- The channel functions primarily as a public status page and trust/reassurance surface, with support and Play acquisition links attached to most posts.
- Between 2026-01-07 and 2026-03-13, the visible sequence contains seven incident/maintenance announcements and six explicit restoration updates. This is transparent operational communication, but it also documents recurring instability.
- No visible post announces a numbered app version or provides a release changelog. The channel therefore does not establish Android release cadence.
- The June campaign is not a neutral review request: reward eligibility explicitly requires five stars. Current Google Play policy prohibits rewards or incentives for ratings/reviews and lists a discount/reward for a high rating as a common violation. This is a material acquisition-policy risk, not a tactic POKROV should copy.
