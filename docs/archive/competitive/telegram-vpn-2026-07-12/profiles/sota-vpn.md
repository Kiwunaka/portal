## Product

The official site ties Telegram management to the Sota Connect application. Google Play package `org.interhive.sota.connect` uses a legacy Interhive namespace, but the store support domain/email and official Sota site confirm the current relationship. Publisher is Cat Connect Oy.

## Assessment

Sota is the strongest large-channel growth candidate after HitVPN in absolute terms and the fastest in relative terms among the biggest app-backed services. It deserves a dedicated future bot/app binary pass; this run did not activate its bot or retrieve the Play-only APK.
