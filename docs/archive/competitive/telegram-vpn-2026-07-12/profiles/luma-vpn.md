## Install Matrix

- iOS: INCY and Happ; Happ was marked unavailable in the RU App Store.
- Android: Happ via Google Play or APK.
- macOS: INCY or Happ, Apple Silicon and Intel.
- Windows: Happ.
- Android TV: Happ via Google Play or APK, RU/EN instructions.
- Apple TV: INCY or Happ via App Store.
- Linux was not offered.

## Payment and Referral

- Platega: SBP, card and crypto.
- YooMoney reserve checkout.
- Telegram Stars.
- Invoices were created, but no payment was completed.
- Referral reward: 20% in points from every invited user's purchase and renewal.
- Points are redeemable for subscription time; QR, share and copy are supported.
- Referral-link reset is immediate, has no confirmation and invalidates the old link.
- PROXY200 was expired during the pass.

## Messaging and Growth

- Claims: from 99 ₽/month, three-day trial, whitelist/jamming/UAV mode, ad-free YouTube and Russian apps remaining available.
- Channel themes: balancer polls, DDoS, infrastructure compromise, rollback, proxy-versus-VPN education, 200K+ proxy users, new nodes and RKN migration.
- TGStat month growth is roughly 200% of the reconstructed starting base.
- Average reach is 6.4 times the subscriber count. This is proxy/repost distribution, not normal subscriber engagement.

## Strengths

- Strongest proxy-to-paid acquisition funnel in the sample.
- Fully functional bot after legal acceptance.
- Clear three-tier packaging and broad consumer-device coverage.
- High off-channel distribution.

## Weaknesses

- TGStat metrics cannot be ranked beside normal channels.
- No Linux client in the direct install matrix.
- Immediate referral-link reset is a destructive UX without confirmation.
- Reliance on proxy infrastructure creates forced migrations and operational risk.

## Implications for POKROV

Test a clearly separated free recovery/proxy funnel, but keep paid-product analytics distinct. Require confirmation before rotating a referral or subscription identity.
