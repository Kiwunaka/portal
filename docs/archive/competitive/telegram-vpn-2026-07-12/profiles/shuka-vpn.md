## Funnel

- Mandatory subscription to `@ShukaVPN` blocks the bot. The gate was passed with user authorization.
- The bot positions around ad-free YouTube, all-device support, privacy, flexible routing and 24 locations.
- The cabinet exposes tariff, invite, gift and payment branches.
- Referral mechanics are unusually concrete: the inviter receives 14 days plus 20 GB of whitelist traffic after the referred user activates a paid referral trial; the new user receives 7 days for 10 ₽.
- Personal referral links were generated during inspection but deliberately not retained.

## Application

Shuka's strongest product asset is not the bot. It is the separate MantaRay client, distributed from a first-party Yandex Cloud object and Google Play. The app has a serious routing builder, widgets, diagnostics, quick settings tile and self-update path. See [MantaRay profile](mantaray.md).

## Content

The only in-window channel post, dated 2026-05-14, bundles new Windows, Android TV and Android apps, new locations, server improvements, a new site, Telegram-independent subscription management and a three-day gift. Low cadence, high release density.

## Assessment

The bot is commercially conventional; the client is not. MantaRay is the best power-user routing UX in this wave. The main weakness is the mandatory channel gate and an ecosystem split between provider, generic client and web cabinet.
