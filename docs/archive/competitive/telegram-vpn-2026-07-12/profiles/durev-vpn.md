## Bot Surface

- Subscription management, purchase, referral, site login, channel, about, help and RU/EN language.
- Purchase options: 17 ₽ / day, 459 ₽ / month, 1,310 ₽ / 3 months, 2,414 ₽ / 6 months, 3,864 ₽ / year, 5,244 ₽ / 2 years, promo code and gift.
- Site login starts by binding an email address. No email was submitted.
- No-subscription management is a direct upsell with a claimed discount up to 58%.
- Knowledge base covers setup, connection incidents, app/service-specific problems and referrals, then hands off to `@DurevVpnSupportBot`.
- Setup guides cover Android, Windows, macOS, iOS, Android TV, Apple TV and Linux. The current flow still uses transferable keys and external-client instructions alongside the branded Android app.

## Referral

- The referred account is permanently attributed after a qualifying purchase.
- Reward can be either free days or money, both at 20% of referred subscription value.
- Referral gifts range from three days to two months.
- Cash payout threshold is 5,000 ₽ / 50 USDT / 3,000 Stars; reward is held 48 hours for refunds.
- Personal referral codes were generated during inspection and intentionally discarded.

## Brand and Risk

Durev explicitly ties the product to the Povel Durev TON meme-token ecosystem and describes it as a symbol of internet freedom. The bot publishes five alternate domains. This is a distinctive acquisition hook, but it mixes VPN trust with a speculative-token brand and a fragmented domain surface.

## Application

- [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.durevpn.durevvpn), package `com.durevpn.durevvpn`, 100K+ installs.
- The official channel calls the current generation Durev 2.0.
- No first-party direct APK or public checksum was available; no mirror APK was accepted.
