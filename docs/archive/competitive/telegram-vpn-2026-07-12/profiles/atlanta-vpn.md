## Complete Observed Flow

1. Mandatory channel subscription and acceptance of a Telegraph user agreement.
2. RU/EN language choice.
3. Three-day free activation and platform choice: iOS, Android, Android TV, Windows or macOS.
4. A named subscription key and private connection URL are issued. The secret URL was not retained.
5. Main menu: buy, profile, setup, invite, diagnostics.
6. Profile: balance, transactions, email, keys, promo and language.
7. Keys: device category, key detail, expiry, whitelist add-on and renewal.
8. Payments: RUB or Heleket crypto. No invoice was paid.
9. Referral and partner programs run in parallel. The bot explicitly warned that partner statistics were malfunctioning during the pass.
10. Support starts with server/country switching and then hands off to `@AtlantaHelp_Bot`.

## Platform Stack

- Android and iOS instructions primarily use Happ.
- Windows uses Happ Desktop from its GitHub release.
- macOS uses V2rayTun.
- Android TV provides a separate remote-control app and a Telegraph guide.
- The channel also promotes an Atlanta App Beta and Google Play package `atlantavpn.bot`, but the main bot's current default path still depends on third-party clients.

## Whitelist Monetization

Atlanta separates ordinary VPN access from traffic-metered “white list bypass.” The base subscription claims eight countries plus four mobile-operator routes. The add-on is presented as the most stable bypass, uses Happ, and is billed per GB. This is a second revenue axis layered on top of time-based subscription pricing.

## Marketing

Five in-window channel posts: one explicit recruitment campaign for traffic acquisition across Telegram, TikTok, Shorts, Reels, YouTube and forums, and three crisis/recovery posts. Atlanta's aggression is affiliate/creator distribution, not just discounts.

## Assessment

Strong commercial mechanics, weak product coherence. The bot monetizes time, traffic, referral and partner channels well, but the user journey crosses Happ, V2rayTun, Telegraph, video channels, a beta app and a separate support bot.
