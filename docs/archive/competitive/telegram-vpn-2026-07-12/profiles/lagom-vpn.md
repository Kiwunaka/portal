## Product

Lagom is app-led rather than channel-led. Its Android scale is an order of magnitude beyond most Telegram-first competitors. The official site and offer connect the bot, service and LAGOM PRODUCTS LLC legal identity; the Play package matches that identity.

## Assessment

The useful signal is not just 5M installs, but concurrent +15% channel growth while the app is actively updated. Lagom should be prioritized for a future APK/UI/bot teardown. This pass intentionally did not replace its Play-only binary with a mirror.
