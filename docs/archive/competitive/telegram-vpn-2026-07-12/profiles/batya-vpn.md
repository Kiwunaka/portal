## Bot and Pricing

- Initial screen offers either a five-day trial or an immediate 20% sale.
- The “forever” plan is sold as a family purchase. The offer legally lasts only while the service exists and can provide the service.
- The tested account hit a reproducible bot defect: `/start` attempted registration again and returned “Пользователь уже существует”; `/menu` and `/help` produced no recovery response.
- The landing page says “all devices,” while the offer recommends no more than five simultaneous devices for timed plans.
- The 12-month discount math is inconsistent: `2,988 → 1,899 ₽` is about 36%, not the displayed 54%.

## Applications

- [Android / Android TV](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ms.f2p.batyavpn): package `ms.f2p.batyavpn`, publisher `F2P, OOO`, 1M+ installs, updated in July 2026.
- [iOS / iPadOS / macOS](https://apps.apple.com/ru/app/%D0%B1%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8F-vpn/id6753714734): App ID `6753714734`; public metadata ties it to `Buddy VPN` / `Frederic Hennes Sprl`.
- Windows is advertised as supported, but the public route installs third-party Happ rather than a Batya-branded binary.
- No trustworthy first-party direct Android APK was exposed. The Play-only binary was not substituted with an unverified mirror.

## Privacy Conflict

Google Play and App Store self-declarations say no data is collected. Batya's own policy says the app and bot process Telegram ID, traffic volume, device type, OS and app version; the site additionally processes IP, cookies, browser fingerprint and behavior for marketing. Store labels should not be accepted without binary/network verification.

## Marketing

- 32 dated events in the two-month window; 21 were tied to giveaways, streams or results.
- One campaign offered 199 prizes including MacBook Air, iPhones, AirPods, money and subscriptions to paying users.
- A later acquisition draw offered 10 iPhones, 10 AirPods and ten 10,000 ₽ payments for channel participation.
- Reviews are incentivized: +14 subscription days for an App Store or Google Play review submitted through a separate review bot.
- Five fear/newsjacking posts used blocks, foreign traffic pricing, MAX and Happ removal to push long plans.

## Assessment

Batya has excellent brand voice and store distribution, but its growth metrics are contaminated by prize traffic and review incentives. The privacy declarations, publisher identities and “forever”/discount copy need skeptical treatment.
