## Product and Funnel

- The public site routes installation and account management through Telegram.
- The bot was not merely degraded: on the live pass it returned “Сервис на обслуживании” and deferred recovery updates to `@hitvpn_news`.
- The support surface shown by the bot was `@hitvpnhelp4`.
- The outage state conflicts with the channel's 2026-06-30 claim that service had been restored. Treat this as observed regression, not proof of permanent downtime.

## Applications

- Official Google Play package: [`io.hvpn.android`](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.hvpn.android), 1M+ installs.
- The recent channel promotes HitRay Android 1.14: [`io.hitray.android`](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.hitray.android), 10M+ installs.
- The first-party site exposed direct APK URL `https://s.hitvpn.tools/4080147.apk`, but the host had an expired TLS certificate and did not deliver a usable binary during the pass. No APK was accepted into evidence.

## Marketing

- Eight of 16 posts in the two-month window were giveaway announcements or results.
- The loop repeats every one to two weeks: subscribe to the channel, connect through the bot, enter an iPhone 17 Pro Max draw, then consume a separate result post.
- Three posts pushed apps; two were crisis communication; the remaining posts were mostly setup reminders.
- On 2026-06-24 the channel said tariffs were stopped and the service made free during a mass block; on 2026-06-30 it claimed recovery and balance preservation.

## Assessment

The scale is real, but the funnel is fragile: it is dominated by prize traffic and a single Telegram control plane. The live maintenance screen is strong evidence that massive reach does not equal operational reliability.
