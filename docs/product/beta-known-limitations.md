# Beta Known Limitations

Last updated: 2026-05-26

- Android is available as an outside-store public beta using owner-attested physical release-build audit evidence; raw device audit proof, store release, and stronger Android safety claims remain manual follow-up.
- Windows builds may remain unsigned and may trigger OS trust warnings.
- Direct download links can be unavailable for users without approved beta access.
- Paid checkout is Lava.top-only in the current public beta; production refund, chargeback, and reconciliation evidence remains follow-up.
- RU-origin reachability was skipped by operator for the beta decision and must be tested separately before any RU-origin readiness claim.
- Support operates best-effort during beta with the cabinet and `@pokrov_supportbot` as official paths.
