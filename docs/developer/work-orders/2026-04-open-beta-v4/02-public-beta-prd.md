## Decision Bias

Release as outside-store public beta while public truth, support, downloads, admin, monitoring, and platform availability are honest and verified. The 2026-05-15 evidence pack authorizes this beta with accepted skips; it does not authorize production or `1.0.0`.

## Beta Scope

- Brand: `POKROV`.
- Public version line: `0.x.x-beta`.
- Public platform scope: Android and Windows only when their gate labels are truthful.
- Apple platforms: readiness only.
- Payments: Lava.top-only paid checkout is enabled for beta where the 2026-05-15 proof applies; production refund/chargeback/reconciliation evidence remains follow-up.
- Android: outside-store public beta may use the owner-attested physical release-build audit; raw device proof remains a manual owner test before stronger claims.
- Windows: public beta artifact requires checksum, explicit unsigned beta warning, and release handoff evidence; trusted signing is a later trust upgrade.
- RU-origin: skipped by operator for this beta; do not claim RU-origin readiness.

## Beta Non-Goals

- No `1.0.0`, app-store, trusted-signing, raw Android-audit, production payment, or RU-origin claim while those separate gates are not proven.
- No public promise for iOS or macOS.
- No raw subscription-link-first commerce story.
- No public wording that uses `VPN` as a direct product description outside legacy or technical identifiers.
